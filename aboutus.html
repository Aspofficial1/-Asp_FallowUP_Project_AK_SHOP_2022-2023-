Our Story

Ak Shop is the biggest shopping center in Sri Lanka, located in the heart of Kurunegala. Founded in 2020, we have grown to become the go-to destination for fashion enthusiasts across the country. Our mission is to provide high-quality, trendy clothing and accessories inspired by global fashion trends, adapted to the Sri Lankan lifestyle.

With a focus on affordability, quality, and customer satisfaction, we offer a wide range of products from ethnic wear to modern casuals, ensuring there's something for everyone. As a proud Sri Lankan business, we support local artisans and promote sustainable fashion practices.
